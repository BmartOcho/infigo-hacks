## Summary
February 2026 release (no version number; harvested 2026-10-01). Insights reports and dashboards can now appear in the storefront's My Account. Also: kits with selectable child attributes, attribute-level inventory on teaser pages, a searchable department picker (for 1000+ departments), and an optional EU VAT number requirement.

## Key takeaways
- **Insights in the storefront** — a new report **Context** setting (Admin / Public Store / both) with role-based permissions. Customers see selected reports in My Account without admin rights. Full screen, auto-refresh, and timezone-aware CSV export. Detail: https://academy.infigo.net/p/3318
- **Kit products with selectable attributes** — child products inherit attribute selections from the kit page and can be edited from the cart. Detail: https://academy.infigo.net/academy/p/3327
- **Attribute inventory on teaser pages** — stock per size/colour shown on teasers. Out-of-stock options are disabled and the price updates live. Detail: https://academy.infigo.net/academy/p/3328
- **Searchable, paginated department selection** across Admin and storefront (full-text search, lazy loading). Detail: https://academy.infigo.net/p/3319
- **EU VAT number required** (setting) — enforced at registration and on account update. Detail: https://academy.infigo.net/p/3320
