## Summary
Two options for hiding prices on the storefront: (1) bulk hide for everyone via a single setting, or (2) custom per-user-type visibility (guest vs. logged-in vs. role-based) which requires an Infigo Support customization request.

## Key takeaways
- Bulk hide for all viewers: **Settings > General Settings > "Hide all pricing on the storefront"** toggle.
- Per-user-type pricing visibility (e.g. show only to logged-in users, hide for guests) is NOT self-serve — must submit a support request to Infigo for custom configuration.
