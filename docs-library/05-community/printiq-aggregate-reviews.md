## Summary
Cross-aggregation of independent PrintIQ user reviews from Slashdot, SourceForge, Capterra (printIQ Core), GetApp, and the PrintPlanet thread. The pattern is unusually consistent across sources and the most important community context our shop can cite when troubleshooting or escalating against PrintIQ.

## Key takeaways
- **Front-end strong, back-end weak (consensus):** "great on the front end of selling you the product and getting in through the implementation process efficiently… not so great on the back end of support. Customer support response time is poor and there are limitations to the software that were unexpected."
- **Implementation is generally smooth** — setup team works at the customer's pace; teams are friendly and print-industry-fluent.
- **Coverage limit benchmark:** a Large Format division pilot reported PrintIQ "covers about 85% of what they need it to do." Plan for 10–20% gap-fill via integrations, scripts, or workarounds.
- **100% cloud, eCommerce built-in (not bolt-on):** competitive distinction PrintIQ leans on vs. legacy MIS.
- **Webinars Infigo + PrintIQ co-host** confirm tight integration is a priced go-to-market motion, not a casual partnership.
- For our shop: when something is broken at the Infigo↔PrintIQ seam, the PrintIQ community signal is that support response time is the bottleneck, so file Infigo + PrintIQ tickets in parallel rather than waiting for hand-off.
