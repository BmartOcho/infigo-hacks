## Summary
PrintPlanet thread where a shop asks for first-hand PrintIQ implementation experience. The thread surfaces a recurring industry sentiment that MIS implementations are universally painful — useful context if our shop is asked to integrate to a new PrintIQ tenant.

## Key takeaways
- Common community sentiment: "it's always painful to implement a new MIS" — not specific to PrintIQ, but the bar.
- Companion Capterra/SoftwareAdvice data shows recurring pattern: **PrintIQ is praised on the front end (sales + implementation) but weaker on back-end ongoing support and unexpected functional limitations** ("covers about 85% of what we need").
- One published evaluation from a Large Format division: "covers about 85% of what they need it to do" — useful expectation-setting for any shop team scoping PrintIQ coverage.
- Sales/implementation pace generally described as smooth and efficient; setup team works at customer pace.
