## Summary
Independent trade-press roundup of 16+ W2P vendors (A-O, with a Part 2 for P-Z) from DPS Magazine, January 2016. Useful as a third-party snapshot of where Infigo's Catfish, MegaEdit, and Symphony sat in the competitive landscape before today's "Connect" + MIS-integration era. Helpful when explaining the shop's stack history or evaluating switching costs.

## Key takeaways
- **Infigo's 2016 positioning per DPS:** "Catfish W2P software platform is an online storefront that allows print organizations to offer new services and products. There is the option of adding templates, enabling customers to edit, manage, and submit print-ready files… Originally created for professional printers to offer enterprise-level photobook and photo merchandise production, Infigo MegaEdit now has another distinct use [as] an online design tool. Symphony is Infigo's latest product. It is a scalable marketing workflow and automation solution comprised of email, SMS, and print."
- **Key competitor framing (still relevant):**
  - **Aleyant Pressero** = SaaS B2B/B2C; DocBuilder adds typography, imposition, scripting, Excel merge.
  - **EFI DigitalStoreFront** = the "established" competitor Catfish users often migrated AWAY from.
  - **Hybrid Software Cloudflow / FRONTDESK / PROOFSCOPE** = workflow-first stack with HTML5 proofing — directly comparable to Infigo's MegaScript + connectors.
  - **Avanti Slingshot, AccuraOnline, Cendix CMP, B2CPrint** = MIS-adjacent W2P that's largely vanished from current conversations.
- Cendix CMP angle ("channel marketing portal, lock down brand messages, channel partners personalize") is essentially the same use case our shop serves with Infigo storefronts for corporate clients.
- MegaEdit's lineage as a **photobook editor** explains why its API is heavy on imagery / layouts / variable substitution — historical context for why VDP tooling lives in MegaEdit, not Infigo Designer.
