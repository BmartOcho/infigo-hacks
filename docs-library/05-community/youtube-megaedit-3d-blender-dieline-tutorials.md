## Summary
Two-part YouTube tutorial covering the workflow for getting a 2D dieline into MegaEdit 3D via Blender — first preparing the dieline in 2D, then adding it to Blender for the 3D preview MegaEdit consumes. Although uploaded to Infigo's own channel, the tutorials are unusually hands-on (Blender steps especially) and are the only public walkthrough of the dieline-to-3D pipeline, so they're worth indexing even though they're not strictly third-party.

## Key takeaways
- Companion videos:
  - "Creating your 2D Dieline or Template for MegaEdit 3D" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhrVu2hTTaM
  - "Adding your 2D Dieline or Template to Blender for MegaEdit 3D" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sigyC4UervA
- Required when building packaging / 3D-preview products in MegaEdit (e.g. folding cartons, custom boxes).
- Blender is the bridge — Infigo expects you (or a partner) to model the carton in Blender, not in MegaEdit directly.
- Related: "Assigning PDF Profiles to MegaEdit Products" (Oct 2024, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3-i72K9bBg) covers the colour-management hand-off for finished MegaEdit products.
- "Template Builder | MegaEdit" playlist — https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLNcsR95lzEpdbsH8VXExprJR_fmfcrE35 — collects the rest of the official video tutorials.
