## Summary
Infigo's own podcast series — vendor-hosted but with external guests (printer ops, sales engineers, support analysts) who speak candidly about implementation, support workflow, and automation. Worth indexing because guest segments are closer to community/operator voice than vendor marketing. Episodes are on Spotify for Podcasters and YouTube Podcasts in addition to Infigo's blog.

## Key takeaways
- **Episode 1 — Successful W2P:** culture/buy-in framing — "successful web-to-print is as much about cultural change and getting buy-in from all stakeholders as technological change." Use this with internal stakeholders.
- **Episode 2 — The Need for Speed (Paul Bromley):** workflow speed levers.
- **Episode 3 — Keep Your Cool.**
- **Episode 5 — Please Raise a Ticket (James Thompson, Infigo Customer Software Support Analyst):** top tips for filing useful support tickets — useful read for anyone at our shop handling Infigo Helpdesk.
- **Episode 7 — Meet the Boss (Alex Bowell).**
- **Imposition Software interview (Julie Watson):** 80% file-prep time reduction claim for MegaScript-driven imposition — useful number for ROI conversations.
- **General workflow tip surfaced:** "Using a dashboard that updates automatically at every stage allows customers to log in and see exactly what's happening, resulting in a huge reduction in 'where's my order?' queries." — direct rationale for keeping Infigo's order-status dashboard exposed to clients.
