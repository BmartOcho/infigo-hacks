## Summary
Long-running PrintPlanet thread (multiple pages) where print operators recommend and complain about W2P platforms. Useful as a community cross-reference when evaluating alternatives or articulating why our shop stays on Infigo. Notably, Infigo/Catfish does not dominate this thread — other platforms (PageFlex, Apogee Storefront, OnPrintShop, OPS, PageDNA) get most of the airtime, suggesting Infigo's installed base is concentrated outside this forum's user pool.

## Key takeaways
- **PageFlex:** "pretty expensive" but solid software and excellent tech support — classic enterprise pricing.
- **Apogee Storefront (Agfa):** strong B2B focus, tight Apogee Prepress workflow integration. Niche but trusted.
- **Online Print Solutions (OPS):** active users 2+ years deep are "very happy" with support/service.
- **OnPrintShop:** 300+ clients / 7+ years, called out for being user-friendly, flexible, and affordable.
- **PageDNA / PrintWise:** also mentioned as serious contenders.
- Recurring meta-point: **trying to stitch standalone tools together is expensive and brittle — vendors with multiple system types ready to work together win.** Validates Infigo's "Connect" strategy.
- Recurring meta-point: for low order volume, W2P alone is enough; MIS makes sense once order volume from online is meaningful.
